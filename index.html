US Passport Photo Sheet Generator

Upload a selfie, adjust the crop, and create a 4" x 6" portrait sheet containing six 2" x 2" passport photos. The result is printable at 300 DPI as a high-quality JPEG.

1. Upload your photo

  • Use a recent color photo taken in the last six months.
  • Pose facing the camera with a neutral expression and open eyes.
  • Ensure the background is plain white or off-white.
  • Remove glasses, hats, and uniforms unless worn for religious purposes.

2. Adjust crop & alignment

Fine tune the face position to meet the required head size.

The gray guides show the safe zone for crown and chin per the official specification (eye level between 1.125" and 1.375" from the bottom).

3. Generate & download

Creates a printable 4x6 layout with six aligned passport photos.

This tool follows the published photo requirements from the United States Department of State but cannot validate biometric details. Carefully review the generated sheet before printing.
